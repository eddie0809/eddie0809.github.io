Github Copilot

2026/11/25

Let me preface this diatribe by saying that Github Copilot is a powerful tool that somewhat helped me with the technical side of this website. I am not a website builder by profession and haven't really touched anything website related for quite some time.

However, not only in regards to this website, but also for README.md files for arrangements, and lecture notes I try stenographically typing out while in the lecture, it is utterly useless. I find myself being mostly annoyed and sometimes insulted by the stuff it suggests. For example, I tried to write an introductory paragrph for recipes earlier today, and it just kept giving me slop output as suggestions. While one could say that this is entirely expected, since it's a coding assistant and not a writing one, I am asking myself, why this is not being detected by the "AI"? Is it not noticing somewhere under the hood that I want my peace with its suggestions and just write?

It even had suggestions for this paragraph I had open here, while I yak-shaved a monospace font from somewhere. This AI craze is getting a bit out of hand.

I feel like this type of writing will never be replaced by AI. Reports about actual happenings. Maybe some rouge AI will one day break out and create a website where it just puts out ramblings about "the stupid humans that don't prompt properly" or something.
